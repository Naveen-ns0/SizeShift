How to resize and compress images

How to resize an image to exact pixels

  1. Open the Image tab and choose your photo.
  2. Type the width you need. The height follows automatically while Keep proportions is ticked.
  3. Pick JPEG for photos, PNG for logos or screenshots, or WebP for the smallest files.
  4. Press Resize, check the preview, then press Download.

How to reduce an image to a size in KB for forms

  1. Choose your photo in the Image tab.
  2. Enter the width and height the form asks for. Read the form's rules first.
  3. Type the maximum size in KB, for example 50 or 100, and select JPEG.
  4. Press Resize. SizeShift lowers quality step by step until the file fits, then shows the final size.
  5. If it says the target was not reached, reduce the pixel size a little and try again.

How to enlarge an image

  1. Choose the image, then tap Double or Triple, or type a bigger width.
  2. Press Resize. The image is enlarged in smooth steps for the best look.
  3. Keep expectations realistic: enlarging helps meet a minimum size but cannot add lost detail.

Worked example: a phone photo of 4000 x 3000 px and 3 MB needs to be 100 KB for an online form. Set the width to 800 (height becomes 600), choose JPEG, enter 100 in the KB box and press Resize. The result is 800 x 600 px at roughly 100 KB or less.

Image terms

Pixel (px)
One dot of an image. Width x height in pixels is the image's size on screen.
KB and MB
File size. 1 MB is 1024 KB. Forms usually limit KB, chat apps usually limit MB.
Aspect ratio
The shape of the picture, like 16:9 wide or 9:16 tall. Keep proportions stops stretching.
JPEG, PNG, WebP
JPEG suits photos, PNG keeps sharp edges and transparency, WebP is usually smallest.

How to shrink or resize a video

How to shrink a video for WhatsApp, Instagram or email

  1. Open the Video tab and choose your video.
  2. Select 480p or 360p for the smallest files, or 720p to keep it sharp.
  3. Set Quality to Low or Smallest for chat apps, Medium for sharing.
  4. Press Convert and keep the tab open and visible until the bar finishes, then download.

Video terms

Resolution
How many pixels a picture or video has. 1080p means 1080 pixels of height.
Aspect ratio
The shape of the picture, like 16:9 wide or 9:16 tall. Keep proportions stops stretching.
Bitrate
How much data each second of video uses. Lower bitrate means a smaller file and less detail.
KB and MB
File size. 1 MB is 1024 KB. Forms usually limit KB, chat apps usually limit MB.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos and videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server.

Is SizeShift free, and do I need to sign up?

It is free and needs no account or login. You can resize as many files as you like.

How do I resize an image to an exact width and height?

Open the Image tab, choose your photo, type the width and height in pixels and press Resize. Untick Keep proportions only if you want to stretch or crop the shape on purpose.

How do I reduce an image to 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB for a form?

Open the Image tab, choose your photo, enter the target size in KB, select JPEG or WebP and press Resize. SizeShift lowers the quality step by step until the file fits. For very small targets, also reduce the pixel size.

Why could SizeShift not reach my target KB?

Some images, such as large or very detailed photos, cannot fit a tiny size at the pixels you chose. Lower the width and height, then try again. PNG ignores the KB target, so use JPEG or WebP.

Does upscaling make a blurry image sharp?

No. Upscaling enlarges the image smoothly but cannot add real detail that was never captured. It is good for meeting minimum pixel requirements, not for restoring old photos.

How much can I enlarge an image before it looks bad?

Doubling usually looks acceptable for photos. Tripling or more starts to look soft, especially on text and small details. Start with Double and compare the preview.

Which format should I choose, JPEG, PNG or WebP?

Use JPEG for photos and for any KB target. Use PNG for logos, screenshots and images that need transparency. Use WebP for small files when the website you upload to accepts it.

How do I make a video smaller for WhatsApp or email?

Open the Video tab, choose your video, select 480p or 360p and a lower quality level, then press Convert. Lower resolution and quality both reduce file size. Chat apps limit file size, and the limit can change, so check the current limit in the app.

Why does video conversion take as long as the video?

The browser re-records the video while playing it, so a 2 minute video takes about 2 minutes. Keep the tab open and visible until it finishes, because background tabs slow down.

Can I turn a low resolution video into HD?

You can choose a larger output height, but the picture is only stretched and gains no real detail. For true sharpness you need a better original recording.

Why is my converted video bigger than the original?

The original may have been compressed more efficiently. Choose a lower Quality setting or a smaller output height, and the new file will shrink.

What size should I use for Instagram posts and stories?

Feed posts commonly look best around 1080 pixels wide. Stories and reels use a tall 9:16 shape, commonly 1080 x 1920 px. Platforms change their guidance, so check the app for current recommendations.

What size should I use for YouTube videos and thumbnails?

Videos are normally wide 16:9, and 1080p or 720p is common. Thumbnails are commonly 1280 x 720 px and must stay under the file size limit YouTube shows when you upload.

What size should I use for Facebook, X and LinkedIn images?

Shared link images are commonly about 1200 x 630 px on Facebook and about 1200 x 627 px on LinkedIn. Wide images on X commonly use a 16:9 shape such as 1600 x 900 px. Check each platform for its latest numbers.

What size should I use for WhatsApp profile photos and status?

Profile photos work best as a square, for example 500 x 500 px or larger. Status videos and images use a tall 9:16 shape such as 1080 x 1920 px. WhatsApp compresses files again after you send them.

How do I resize a photo for an exam, passport or government form?

Each form states its own pixel size, KB limit and file type, so read those rules first. Enter the width and height, add the KB limit, choose JPEG and press Resize. Then confirm the final size shown before uploading.

Does resizing change my original file?

No. Your original file stays untouched on your device. SizeShift creates a new file that you download.

Will SizeShift work on my phone?

Images work on modern phones. Video conversion works best on desktop Chrome or Edge, and large videos can fail on phones because of limited memory.

Which browsers work best?

Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari work for images. Video conversion works best on desktop Chrome and Edge.
